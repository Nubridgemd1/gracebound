Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Is this medical or nursing care?

No. Gracebound provides non-medical personal assistance — help with daily living, companionship, homemaking and similar support. We are not a licensed medical, nursing or home-health agency, and our caregivers do not provide medical treatment or administer medication. For medical needs, we're happy to work alongside your healthcare providers.

Where is care provided?

In your own home or residence — we come to you. There is no facility to move into. Care can be provided in a house, apartment, a family member's home, or an assisted-living residence.

Are your caregivers screened?

Yes. Caregivers are background-checked, carefully interviewed and trained, and we are insured and bonded. We also match you with a caregiver suited to your needs and personality.

Is there a minimum number of hours?

We keep it flexible. Whether you need a few hours a week or around-the-clock support, we'll build a plan that fits. We'll go over any minimums during your free consultation.

Can we choose or change our caregiver?

Absolutely. Your comfort matters — if a match isn't quite right, just let us know and we'll arrange a change.

How do we pay?

Most families pay privately, and long-term care insurance may apply. We'll explain simple, transparent options and build a plan that fits your budget.

How do we get started?

Call (201) 349-3486 or send the request form below. We'll schedule a free in-home visit, answer your questions, and create a care plan together.
